1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At EmailLink, we prioritize your privacy. This tool is designed as a client-side utility, meaning the generation of your mailto links happens entirely within your web browser. We do not store, collect, or transmit any email addresses or personal data entered into our generator to any external servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because the "EmailLink Generator" runs locally on your machine, we do not have access to:
